# Batch 44 — adaptive quiet-region dialogue attenuation

Optional offline CPU-only level-based noise reduction with four modes: off (default), light (minimum 70% amplitude), medium (50%) and strong (35%). The algorithm estimates a quiet floor from full 320-sample (20 ms) windows per source turn, using integer mean-square energy and a low-percentile order statistic. Requires at least ten complete windows, floor equivalent to 16–2000 PCM RMS, and adequate higher-energy contrast. Bypasses silence, short segments and insufficient evidence. Low-energy windows at or below twice floor energy use the selected minimum gain; windows at nine times floor or higher use unity, with interpolation between thresholds. Five-ms (80 samples) transition between window gains. Reset state per turn and pause. Exact frame count, zero samples and polarity preserved; no amplification by this stage, no new dependencies. The optional stage follows DC and targeted filters, before speech peak normalization. Music/intro/outro and original master are unchanged.

CLI: `project-mix-compose --project-id ID --adaptive-noise medium --acknowledgment I_HAVE_RIGHTS_TO_ALL_ADDED_AUDIO_AND_AUTHORIZE_LOCAL_MIX`. Browser selector matches CLI. Immutable receipt binds the algorithm, mode, frame/ramp sizes and minimum gain. Older requests and receipts are unchanged when off.

This is adaptive broadband level attenuation, not spectral source separation or speech recognition. It does not remove noise underneath louder speech; uniform or insufficiently contrasted signals are bypassed. Quiet speech may be mistaken for noise. Peak normalization may amplify remaining noise. Listening is required before approving any mix; no speech quality claim.
